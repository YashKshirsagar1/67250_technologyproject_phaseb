Welcome to the MonoMuse Museum

MonoMuse is a contemporary museum dedicated to exploring the richness and diversity of Asian art, culture, and history. Through immersive exhibitions, traditional and modern installations, and rotating collections, MonoMuse invites visitors to discover the artistic traditions of East, South, and Southeast Asia.

Explore exhibitions showing traditional and contemporary Asian art from across diverse cultures and time periods.

MonoMuse features curated collections including calligraphy, ceramics, textiles, paintings, and modern Asian art. Visitors can attend cultural workshops, artist talks, and discussions that deepen their understanding of Asian heritage and artistic practices.
